Rules enforced by code, not by the AI

  • An order is shown only when the order number and the email on it both match.
  • Replies are blocked if they mention an order or tracking number that wasn't verified in this chat.
  • Replies are blocked if they claim a refund, cancellation or change the bot can't make.
  • Refunds, damaged or missing items, and requests for a person always go to a human.
